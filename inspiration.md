# VD BARRISOL Inspiration

Last updated: 2026-06-06

## Positioning

VD BARRISOL should feel like a premium ceiling and interior finish company, not a generic renovation contractor.

The site should communicate:

- Clean execution.
- High-end interiors.
- Light, texture, and finish quality.
- Trust and clarity.
- Fast contact path.

## Visual Direction

Use the brand orange and black as anchors, but avoid a one-note orange/black UI.

Recommended balance:

- Black or near-black for typography, footer, navigation accents.
- Orange for CTA, active states, key price highlights.
- Warm neutral backgrounds to support interior photography.
- White/soft grey surfaces where forms and calculators need clarity.

Avoid:

- Huge generic construction hero.
- Overused dark luxury gradients.
- Random decorative shapes.
- Too much orange background.
- Carousel motion that distracts from the actual ceilings.

## Hero Direction

The hero should be image-led:

- Use real project photography.
- First image should be the strongest ceiling/interior shot.
- Rotate slowly after page load.
- Keep text minimal.
- Include direct CTAs: WhatsApp and estimate.
- Make the product obvious in the first viewport.

If the available photos are weak, the design must compensate with cropping, color correction, and layout discipline, not fake stock imagery.

## Calculator Direction

The calculator should feel like a professional estimator:

- Square meters field.
- Ceiling/product type.
- Lighting options.
- Complexity/shape.
- City/transport if needed.
- Price range in RON and EUR.
- Clear approximate disclaimer.
- CTA to send estimate via form or WhatsApp.

Do not overfit the first version. Better to ship a clean estimate range than a fake-precise number.

## Gallery Direction

The gallery should sell trust:

- Projects grouped by strongest images.
- City/location where useful.
- Area and type when available.
- Testimonials as editorial blocks, not tiny star cards.
- Avoid masonry if it causes layout instability; use a disciplined responsive grid.

## About Direction

About should answer:

- Who they are.
- Where they work.
- Why stretch ceilings / Barrisol-type systems are premium.
- What clients can expect.
- How to request an estimate.

Keep it concise. The gallery and photos should do much of the selling.
